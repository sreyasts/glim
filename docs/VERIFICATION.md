# Verification — 1–2 November 2026

- `npm run check`: 29 tests passed; TypeScript and production build passed. Tests cover ownership, origin checks, persistence, alerts, provider parsing, country filtering, antimeridian bounds, cold deep links, request coalescing, HLS rewriting, and secret exclusion from catalog responses.
- `npm audit`: zero vulnerabilities after updating Fastify static and overriding its transitive glob dependency to patched 14.0.3. Static serving is checked separately because this crosses a dependency major version.
- A random sample of 100 Caltrans entries across ten districts advertising HLS received video segment bytes from 69; 68 had advancing playlist windows. Twenty-six playlists and five segments returned 404. This is a source-network check, **not 100 browser playback verifications**. Reproduce with `npx tsx scripts/check-live-sources.ts 100`; results go to ignored `artifacts/live-source-health.json`.
- Actual HLS playback was observed in Chico, San Diego, Oakland, Carmel, and Stockton. Measured examples: Chico advanced from 7.77 to 25.24 seconds; Oakland reached 49.25 seconds at 720px width; Carmel reached 33.98 seconds at 896px width; Stockton reached 9.13 seconds at 720px width. These measurements had unpaused video and readyState 4.
- Fresno showed a stream failure; Van Nuys and Devore showed connection timeouts. Recovery controls were inspected. Eureka opened but was not conclusively verified. The player now prefers hls.js, starts after manifest parsing, limits retries, exposes a 25-second startup timeout, and offers retry, image, and nearby live-camera recovery. Failed streams are labeled unavailable in the current browser session.
- Kannur, India, decoded the owner-published YouTube live broadcast at 854×480 with a current source timestamp. Abbey Road, London, decoded at 854×480. Sheung Shui, Hong Kong, decoded at 704×576. Windy's actual stream page is now embedded instead of its clickable preview. Bengaluru did not load and Muizenberg's nested player failed; an opened iframe is never labeled as confirmed playback.
- The old Prague video had ended and was removed. Current YouTube broadcast metadata was checked for Kannur, Abbey Road, Venice, and Tembe. A broadcast flag cannot prove every frame is current: operators may show delays, alternate views, or labeled highlights.
- Map navigation, clusters, camera selection at zoom 14.5, and inline playback were checked in desktop and mobile layouts. MapLibre's worker URL is configured explicitly. Map attribution no longer overlays the mobile viewer. Navigation has accessible names.

Not verified end-to-end: every indexed camera, all countries, sustained uptime, background Web Push delivery on a consenting device, Docker runtime, search indexing, production load, or user acquisition. The 100-source sample cannot establish worldwide reliability. See [coverage](COVERAGE.md).

MapLibre, its worker, and HLS load separately. The map chunk is approximately 327 KB gzip, its worker 518 KB uncompressed, and HLS 179 KB gzip. These are material downloads on slow devices; the camera list does not require the map bundle to finish.
